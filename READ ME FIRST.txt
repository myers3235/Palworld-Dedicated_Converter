PALWORLD SAVE CONVERTER

Moves your world off a rented dedicated server onto your own PC.


START HERE
    Double-click:   Run Palworld Save Converter.cmd

If Windows says "Windows protected your PC", click More info, then Run anyway.


THE ONE THING PEOPLE GET WRONG

The converter website works in both directions, and it opens in the WRONG
one. Before you do anything on that page, click "Dedicated to Co-op" in the
top right of the Save converter box.

You know you got it right when Step 1 says "Choose your dedicated server
save". If it says "Choose your co-op save", or asks you for a "dedicated
player ID" or a SteamID64, you are in the wrong direction - switch it and
start that page over.

In the correct direction there is no player ID to enter at all. You pick
the folder, pick your character, and convert.


IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG

    "The selected character already uses 308722BA0000..."
        Wrong direction. See above.

    It cannot find my download
        Click "Find it myself" and point it at the zip, or drag the zip
        onto the box. You do not need to unzip it first.

    "Palworld has not made a save on this PC yet"
        Launch the game, load any world, quit, then click Change.

    The world is missing from the game
        It went into the folder named with a 17-digit number - the last
        screen has a button that opens it.

    The map is black / my explored area is gone
        Map discovery is stored per player in LocalData.sav and is not
        part of the world. Copy LocalData.sav from the world folder you
        used while playing on the server into the new world folder.

    My pals or bases look wrong
        Do not keep playing. Delete the imported world folder and convert
        again from your original download - that is why you keep it.


Keep both files together in the same folder.
